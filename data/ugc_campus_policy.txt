UNIVERSITY GRANTS COMMISSION (UGC) & CAMPUS CONDUCT NORMS

1. Prohibition of Ragging and Harassment:
In accordance with UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, ragging in any form is strictly prohibited within and outside the university/campus premises. Ragging constitutes any disorderly conduct, teasing, treating with rudeness, physical or psychological abuse, extortion, or causing annoyance/apprehension to any student. Zero tolerance is maintained; violators are subject to immediate suspension, rustication, and criminal prosecution under Indian Penal Code (IPC) provisions.

2. Substance Abuse, Alcohol, and Contraband Policy:
Higher educational institutions governed by UGC regulations maintain a strict dry and substance-free campus policy. Possession, consumption, distribution, or storage of alcoholic beverages, illegal drugs, narcotics, psychotropic substances, and tobacco products within academic blocks, hostels, residential areas, sports complexes, and official campus transport is strictly illegal and prohibited. Bringing unauthorized contraband onto campus grounds is grounds for immediate disciplinary expulsion.

3. Campus Security, Access Control, and Curfew Rules:
Students must adhere to institutional entry and exit timings, biometric attendance protocols, and hostel curfew guidelines. Unauthorized entry of outsiders, visitors, or unregistered guests into hostel rooms or academic zones without prior written permission from the warden or competent authority is forbidden. Bypassing security checkposts, scaling compound walls, or forging gate passes is a severe breach of disciplinary code.

4. Academic Integrity and Intellectual Property:
Students must uphold strict academic honesty. Plagiarism, ghostwriting, unauthorized data fabrication, AI-generated academic fraud, or submitting stolen/unattributed coursework for grading violates institutional ethics and examination bye-laws.

5. Use of University IT Infrastructure:
Students using campus Wi-Fi, laboratory computers, or university servers are prohibited from engaging in cyberbullying, unauthorized network scraping, deploying malware, torrenting copyrighted material, bypassing campus firewalls, or accessing restricted adult/malicious content on institutional networks.
